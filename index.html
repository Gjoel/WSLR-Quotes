RMS live link

checking…

The quote server talks to RMS Cloud on this tool's behalf (credentials stay on the server). You do not need this page to get rates: pressing ↻ Live from RMS on the Prepare tab sets all of this up by itself the first time, including matching the cabin types below. Come here only to check or correct that matching, to pick a different rate type, or to turn pencil bookings on. Every organiser page opens with today's availability, the Pipeline flags quotes RMS can no longer cover, and a submitted quote is pencilled into RMS as one group booking.

Press Load from RMS to list the categories and rooms, then match each cabin and campsite type to its RMS category, and each venue to its specific room. Types left as "not in RMS" keep their rate card price and are never pencilled.

Cabins and sites take their nightly price and unit counts from RMS. A venue mapped to a specific room keeps its full-day and half-day price from the rate card (the RMS rate is per category, so every room in "Conference Room" would otherwise price the same); RMS is only asked whether that room is free, and that room is what gets pencilled.

Guard rails: prices already on a sent quote never change on their own (only the Live from RMS button, on the Prepare tab, replaces them). Availability changes warn and cap the organiser's steppers; they never rewrite a quote. A pencil is written once per submission, only if every unit is still free, and only ever cancelled by this tool while it is still a pencil in RMS. Everything it does is emailed to enquiries@.

Email subject line

Used by the Email quote button. You can use {number}, {event}, {org} and {contact}.

Email message

Prefilled into the email; you can still edit every email before sending. {link} becomes the client's quote link, and {number}, {event}, {org}, {contact} and {prepBy} fill themselves in.
Whole sentences that write themselves from the quote, and disappear when they don't apply: {stayline} the dates, nights and guests · {roomsline} how many rooms you prefilled and how to change them · {cateringline} the meals included · {venueline} the rooms booked · {churchline} the 20% / 50% concessional rates, only for a group ticked as a church or ACNC charity.
Bare values, if you would rather write the sentence yourself: {dates}, {nights}, {guests}, {rooms}, {total}.
