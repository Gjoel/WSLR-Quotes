    RMS live link

    checking…

    The quote server talks to RMS Cloud on this tool's behalf (credentials stay on the server). You do not need this page to get rates: pressing ↻ Live from RMS on the Prepare tab sets all of this up by itself the first time, including matching the cabin types below. Come here only to check or correct that matching, to pick a different rate type, or to turn RMS bookings on. Every organiser page opens with today's availability, the Pipeline flags quotes RMS can no longer cover, and a submitted quote is booked into RMS as one unconfirmed group reservation with a deposit invoice emailed to the organiser.

    Press Load from RMS to list the categories and rooms, then match each cabin and campsite type to its RMS category, and each venue to its specific room. Types left as "not in RMS" keep their rate card price and are never booked into RMS.

    Cabins and sites take their nightly price and unit counts from RMS. A venue is one specific room in RMS: it keeps its full-day and half-day price from the rate card (the RMS rate is per category, so every room in "Conference Room" would otherwise price the same), RMS is asked day by day whether that room is free (so a room is ever only 1 free or 0, never a count), and that room is what gets booked. Conference Rooms 1, 2 & 3 together have no room of their own in RMS: map Room 1, Room 2 and Room 3 and the combined space follows, free only when all three are, and a combined booking books all three.

    Guard rails: prices already on a sent quote never change on their own (only the Live from RMS button, on the Prepare tab, replaces them). Availability changes warn and cap the organiser's steppers; they never rewrite a quote. A reservation is written once per submission for whatever is still free (the rest is emailed to you), and is only ever cancelled by this tool while it is still unconfirmed in RMS. Nothing is cancelled automatically for a late deposit: you get a reminder and decide. Everything it does is emailed to enquiries@.
