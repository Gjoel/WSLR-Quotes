from Kate's menu selection form, untick anything not on offer for this event. They can't order from it; it shows what they could get.

Church & charitable discount

Ticked: the 20%/50% concessional conditions appear on the quote and the organiser can pick their tier, choosing 50% without at least 3 group-catered main meals (with everyone staying onsite catered for) explains why it doesn't qualify. The discount applies to accommodation and venue hire only, never catering. Unticked: no church content appears anywhere.
